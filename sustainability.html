Reforestation Initiative

Tree Plantation Program

Pakistan has experienced significant deforestation over the past decades. As a company that depends on the health of our natural environment, Zarrar Adventures is committed to active reforestation efforts across the regions where we operate.

For every expedition we run, we plant trees in partnership with local communities and the government's Billion Tree Tsunami initiative. Our plantation drives in Swat, Kaghan, Hunza, and Baltistan have contributed thousands of saplings to restored forests.


🌳

1 Trip = 10 Trees

Every expedition booking automatically funds 10 trees planted in the departure region.

🤝

Community-Led Planting

We hire local villagers for planting and maintenance, creating income while restoring habitat.

📊

Tracked & Verified

All plantings are geo-tagged and monitored to ensure survival rates and long-term growth.

Our Commitments

Environmental Responsibility

These are the principles we enforce on every single expedition — without exception.

🚫

Zero Plastic Policy

All single-use plastics are banned on our expeditions. We provide reusable water bottles, containers, and biodegradable packaging to every participant.

🗑️

Leave No Trace

Every camp is left cleaner than we found it. We conduct mandatory post-camp cleanup checks and carry all waste back with us. No exceptions.

🔥

Responsible Campfires

Campfires only in designated fire rings using dead wood. We educate all participants on fire safety and ecosystem impact before departure.

🦅

Wildlife Protection

Strict no-feeding, no-disturbing, no-approaching wildlife policies. We work with WWF-Pakistan on wildlife corridor protection initiatives.

🏘️

Community Support

70% of our guides and support staff are hired from local mountain communities, ensuring tourism income stays in the regions we explore.

💧

Water Stewardship

We use and promote water filtration systems to protect water sources. No soap or chemicals are used within 200m of any natural water body.

Join Our Next Cleanup Drive

Our quarterly cleanup expeditions combine adventure with purpose. Come trek with us and help carry out what others have left behind. Free of charge for all volunteers.
